In one breath

"Icebreaker" turns a LinkedIn-style profile into a chatbot you can interview. It is the only folder in this repo with a proper layered design: main.py orchestrates, modules/ does the work, config.py holds every tunable value. Read it as the grown-up version of the single-file demos elsewhere.

What each file does

  • main.py - flags, pipeline order, chat loop.
  • config.py - ids, sizes, prompts.
  • modules/data_extraction.py - fetch and clean a profile.
  • modules/data_processing.py - JSON to nodes to vector index.
  • modules/query_engine.py - the two LLM calls.
  • modules/llm_interface.py - model factories.
  • modules/__init__.py - package exports.
  • profile_lookup.py - a standalone HTTP demo.
  • test_config.py - a 16-line sanity check.

Think of a wedding "ice-breaker" host

You are at a family function and do not know the other guests. A good host reads the invitation card (the profile), picks three genuinely interesting details ("she studied in Pune, worked at a fintech, and volunteers on weekends"), and then answers your follow-up questions from the card - never inventing a detail to keep the conversation going.

  • Reading the card = extraction, then chunking, then embedding.
  • Three facts = generate_initial_facts() with the facts template.
  • Follow-up questions = answer_user_query() with the question template.
  • "I don't know" = the exact sentence the template tells the model to use when the card is silent.
